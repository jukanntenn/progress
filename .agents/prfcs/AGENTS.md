# AGENTS.md — PRFCs

PRFCs are progress's RFCs: durable proposals and decision records preserving the why, the alternatives that lost, and what code and specs cannot carry. The full contract lives in the [PRFC rules](README.md); the repo [documentation standard](../../docs/AGENTS.md) governs every document.

**Every new PRFC triggers a duplicate check** ([scope](README.md#when-to-write-one)): the decision may already have a home in this tree.

**Never edit a PRFC into a different decision** — supersede it with a new PRFC and cross-link both ([lifecycle rules](README.md#layout-and-naming)).

**Every record is a bilingual pair** — write `foo.md` and `foo.zh.md` together, machine tokens and section headings in English, and update the pair together ([format rules](README.md#the-file-format)).
